## Purpose and evidence limits

Use disposable synthetic references in the retained local Docker stack. Preserve every existing account, quest, completion, mailbox fixture and ignored artifact. Print only case names, result categories and snapshot equality. Never record credentials, codes, bearer references, correspondence, exact birthdays or documents in this report or driver output.

An operator disposition about applicable own consent is distinct from guardian authorization. A teen does not need a guardian merely because of age. The operator must justify the applicable arrangement; automation cannot supply that judgment. When authority is required, confirming two contact endpoints provides contact-control evidence only. Neither email linking, a reply nor an adult declaration independently proves parental responsibility.
